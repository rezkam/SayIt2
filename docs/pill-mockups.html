SayIt 2: how should the pill show your words?

The text field now gets nothing until you stop; then the whole text arrives at once. While you speak, the words live in the indicator just above the Dock. Five ways to do that, shown at actual size. Play the loop, or step through it, and try a long dictation and Persian.

Concept A · what I had built

Growing Pill

One black shape. It starts as the small pill and widens with the words; after one line it wraps and grows upward, up to four lines, keeping the newest words.

Feels like
One object that fills up as you talk.
Downside
The shape keeps changing size while you speak.
Concept B

Pill and Caption Card

The small pill stays exactly as it is now. Above it, a separate dark card holds the words, up to three lines, slightly larger text.

Feels like
The recording light stays put; the words float above it.
Downside
Two shapes instead of one.
Concept C

Subtitle Strip

A fixed-width strip like film subtitles: bars on top, two centered lines of words underneath. It never changes size; old lines roll off.

Feels like
Captions on a video. Easy to read at a glance.
Downside
Biggest footprint, even for one word.
Concept D

Two-Line Ticker

A pill of fixed size with a two-line window. New words appear at the bottom and older lines scroll up and fade out the top.

Feels like
A calm, steady pill; the text moves, the shape does not.
Downside
Only about two lines of context.
Concept E · lightest

Floating Words

A smaller pill, and the words float above it with no background at all, just a soft shadow so they read on any window.

Feels like
Almost nothing on screen.
Downside
Harder to read over busy or bright content.
